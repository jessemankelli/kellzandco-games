Sneaker Sprint Lab: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Developer: KellzAndCo

Contact: kellzbabi22@outlook.com

Sneaker Sprint Lab is a racing and sneaker-building game made for kids aged 8 to 14.

The short version

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat and no links to websites or other apps.
  • The game works fully offline. It does not send anything over the internet.

What the game stores on the device

The game saves your progress on your own device only: sparks earned, parts you unlocked, medals, your sneaker design, and settings such as music, sounds, vibration and Calm Mode. This information never leaves the device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app, or using Settings > Hold 3 sec to reset progress, removes it.

Children's privacy (COPPA and similar laws)

The game is directed to children. Because it does not collect any personal information from anyone, as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws (such as the GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code), no parental consent is needed. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or any software development kit (SDK) that collects data.

Permissions

The Android version uses the standard "vibrate" permission for optional haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings. The game does not ask for the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or internet access.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the effective date. We will never start collecting personal information from children without first meeting the legal requirements, including verifiable parental consent where required.
