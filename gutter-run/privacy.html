Gutter Run: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

Developer: KellzAndCo

Contact: kellzbabi22@outlook.com

Gutter Run ("the game") is an offline arcade game for iPhone and Android made by KellzAndCo ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: we do not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has:

  • no user accounts or sign-in
  • no advertising and no ad SDKs
  • no analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools
  • no in-app purchases
  • no chat, social features or user-generated content
  • no access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files

The game does not need an internet connection and does not send any data over the network.

Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a small amount of game data only on your device:

  • your best scores, which stages are unlocked and finished, and whether you've seen the tutorial
  • your settings (music, sound effects, mute and vibration on/off)

This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game. Depending on your device settings, your phone's own system backup (for example iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup) may include it, under Apple's or Google's privacy policies. That backup is not sent to us.

Vibration

On devices that support it, the game can vibrate briefly when your raindrop gets hit. You can switch this off in Settings. Vibration does not involve any data.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Third parties

We do not share data with third parties, because we don't collect any. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) process your purchase and download under their own privacy policies. We receive only aggregated, anonymous sales and download reports from them, which do not identify you.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. To remove the game data stored on your device, uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this policy and its effective date before that change is released.

Contact
